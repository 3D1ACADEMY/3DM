Every provider below gives you a usable key without paying. Most never ask for a card. Limits are typical values as of October 2026 — always confirm on the provider's pricing page before you build on one.

## No key at all

Start here if you just want to try something. These need no signup:

- **Weather** — Open-Meteo, MET Norway, National Weather Service, wttr.in
- **Geo** — Nominatim and Overpass (OpenStreetMap), Zippopotam.us, REST Countries
- **Finance** — CoinPaprika, Binance market data, mempool.space, Frankfurter (FX), SEC EDGAR
- **Reference** — Wikipedia/Wikimedia REST, Open Library, Gutendex, Internet Archive, Library of Congress
- **Fun & data** — Hacker News, Bluesky public API, TVmaze, Jikan, AniList, SWAPI, PokeAPI, Open Trivia DB, Dog CEO, iTunes Search, MusicBrainz, Deezer, Radio Browser

## Free tier, but a card or trial is involved

- **Cloud credits** — Google Cloud, Azure, AWS (limited-time free credits).
- **One-time trial credit** — Replicate, Fireworks AI, Nebius, Novita, Scaleway, Cerebras.
- **Card required for verification** — Google Maps Platform ($200/mo credit), many finance and geocoding APIs.
- **SMS activation** — some SMS, scraping, and proxy APIs require a verified phone number.

<Warning>
  Free tiers change without notice, and many are non-commercial or development-only. Read the terms before you ship, keep your key in an environment variable, and never commit it to git.
</Warning>
